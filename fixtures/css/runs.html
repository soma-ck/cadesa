High water reaches 4.2 m1 at the outer mole, and the channel keeps a depth of 3 m below chart datum2 through the spring tides, when the current runs at two knots past the beacon.

Boats leaving before dawn carry a lantern required and report to the pilot station on channel 12, then hold clear of the ferry lane until it signals.

The lock opens at 05:40 and 17:10; the bridge lifts on the hour and on request, with a horn before it moves and a bell after it closes, every day of the season.

Moorings marked are free, those marked are reserved, and a means the berth has power and water.
