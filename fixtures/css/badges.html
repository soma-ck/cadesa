Allotment rota

Water the beans 1 before nine, then the squash 2 at noon.

Weed the onion bed 3 and the herb border 4 on Saturday.

Turn the compost 5 every second week.

Check the netting Mon and the ties Thu after wind.

Sow the radishes A in rows B a hand apart, and thin them once the second leaves show.

Harvest C in the morning, D before the sun is high.

Mark the gaps G where seed failed and resow them in May.

Label every row E with the date it was sown F and the variety.
