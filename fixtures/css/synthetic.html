Field notes from the orchard cooperative

Harvest week, pressing schedule

The early apples go to the press on Monday, the pears on Wednesday, and anything left over is sorted by hand for the market stall on Saturday morning.

Bring gloves, a knife and a crate of your own.

Volunteers who stay for the whole week get a share of the cider, bottled in the spring.
